Balance — Primary Risk

  • Balance >$1,500 ⇒ 28.3% default rate (911 customers; 258 defaults)
  • $1,000–$1,500 band: 2.5% default rate
  • $500–$1,000 band: 0.2% default rate

Students — Higher Risk

  • Students default at 4.3% vs 2.9% for non-students
  • Students avg balance: $987 vs $772 (non-students)
  • Students avg income: $17,950 vs $40,012 (non-students)

Income Signals

  • Low, Medium, High income bands show similar defaulter counts (24 each)
  • Income alone is not linearly predictive of default
  • Balance-to-income ratio is a stronger predictor

Risk Concentration

  • 97% non-defaulters; defaults concentrated in high-balance bands
  • Targeting >$1,500 balances could address ~77% of defaults
  • Note: $0–$500 band has incomplete data

Portfolio Exposure

  • Non-student portfolio: $5.4M income & $5.4M balance

Sales — Most At-Risk

  • Sales department attrition: 20.63% (HR: 19.05%)
  • R&D lowest at 13.84% despite being the largest team
  • Sales Representatives attrition: 39.76%

Young Employees — Early Exit

  • 18–25 attrition: 35.77% — more than double the company average
  • 26–35 attrition: 19.14%; drops to 9.19% at 36–45
  • Early-career retention programs recommended

Overtime — Burnout Driver

  • Overtime workers show much higher attrition (416 vs 1,054 without)
  • ~28% of overtime workers attrite — strong burnout signal
  • Reducing mandatory overtime is a high-ROI retention option

Satisfaction & WLB

  • Lowest satisfaction (1) attrition: 22.8%
  • WLB score 1 attrition: 31.3% — nearly 2x average
  • WLB=4 attrition: 16.9% — suggests compounding factors

Income & Retention

  • Sales Reps avg pay: $2,600/month — highest attrition
  • Managers/Directors: $16k–17.2k/month with lower attrition
  • Compensation review for bottom-tier earners recommended

Tenure Patterns

  • Attrition spikes in first 1–3 years; past 5 years retention improves
  • Onboarding and early development are pivotal retention levers

Gender Attrition

  • Male: 732 retained vs 150 attrited (~17%)
  • Female: 501 retained vs 87 attrited (~15%)
  • Patterns similar — organization-wide issues

  • Delhi underperforms at $81K — investigate distribution or demand gaps
  • Targeted market interventions recommended for Delhi

Inspection Crisis

  • Only 23% inspections pass — 77% fail or pending
  • 36% actively fail: systemic quality control issues
  • 41% pending: inspection backlog delaying fulfillment
  • Immediate supplier audits and stricter incoming QC required

Supplier Performance

  • Supplier 5 defect rate 2.7%; Supplier 3 at 2.5%; Supplier 1 best at 1.8%
  • Supplier 4: longest lead time (17d) + high defect rate — dual risk
  • Speed vs quality: Supplier 3 fastest (14.33d) but higher defects

Lead Time Risk

  • Avg lead time ~16 days (14.33–17d range) — risky for fast-moving SKUs

Transport & Costs
